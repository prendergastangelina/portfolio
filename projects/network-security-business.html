Network Security Business Model

A business concept for providing remote-work security services to niche organizations with limited internal IT resources.

Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a team to develop a business model and technical solution for Niche Networks for Niche Businesses (N.N.N.B.), a fictional IT service provider focused on helping small and specialized organizations securely transition to hybrid and remote work environments. Together, we designed a service model that combined cybersecurity, networking, cloud technologies, and business strategy to address the real challenges organizations face when supporting a distributed workforce. The project emphasized balancing strong security practices with usability, scalability, and seamless integration into existing IT environments.

Proposed Services

N.N.N.B. was designed to provide organizations with comprehensive networking and cybersecurity solutions tailored to their specific needs. Proposed services included secure remote access through VPNs, identity and access management using Microsoft Entra ID, endpoint management with Microsoft Intune, multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, network segmentation, security monitoring, employee cybersecurity awareness training, and ongoing infrastructure support. The goal was to provide businesses with enterprise-level security while maintaining a user-friendly experience for both employees and existing IT teams.

What I Learned

Working on this project helped me better understand how technical security solutions must also meet business objectives and user needs. Collaborating with a team gave me experience discussing ideas, combining different perspectives, and developing a solution that balanced networking, cybersecurity, cloud technologies, and business strategy. The project reinforced that successful IT solutions require both technical knowledge and effective communication, planning, and teamwork.
